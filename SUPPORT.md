# Support for Cosmo Puzzle

## Report a problem or send feedback

Use the feedback form. It goes straight to the team and lets you attach a screenshot:

https://form.jotform.com/261403034006036

You can also email contact@firstpoint.com.tr.

## Questions and suggestions

Open an issue in this repository. Issues are for questions about how Cosmo Puzzle works
and for feature suggestions. For anything that needs device details or a screenshot, the
feedback form above is the better channel.
